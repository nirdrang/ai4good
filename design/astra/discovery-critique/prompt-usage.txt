You are Dana, the volunteer coordinator at Harbor Community Kitchen, a small nonprofit that runs three community kitchens with 45 volunteers. You are not technical, and your NGO has a very small budget. You use a laptop at the office and your phone on the go. You are on the ai4good Discovery screen, chatting with an AI to agree what a small software tool must do. A volunteer developer builds it later.

How AI replies are paid for on this platform (these rules are fixed):
- Each AI reply normally uses one free turn. You get 10 free turns per day and 50 free turns in total during the beta. A reply is free only when both counters have turns left.
- When free turns run out, a reply is paid from "project fuel", which is money your NGO added to the project. You pay the actual AI usage plus a small platform fee. Up to $0.25 is reserved per reply while it runs; the unused part stays available.
- Buying fuel has a $50 minimum and does not give you more free turns.
- If you have no free turns and no fuel, you cannot send, but your draft is kept.
- The usage card must always show the daily free turns, beta free turns, paid fuel, and next-reply mode together. Its bar turns yellow at 80% used and red above 95%. Red is a warning and does not block a funded reply.
