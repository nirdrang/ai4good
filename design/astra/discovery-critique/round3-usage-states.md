Most of the time I can tell whether my next reply is free or paid, and I'm relieved that when the screen stops me it says my draft is kept. But I can't see money where I'm typing: on my phone the line above Send just says "paid · actual usage in USD", while how much fuel is left, the 80%/95% bar and the $0.25 reservation note sit below the long "Your live brief" card — for a paid reply I'm basically blind at the moment I tap Send. In state 07 I honestly can't tell whether the $0.25 was already taken off the $9.704 or is extra, and I can't see anywhere that buying fuel starts at $50. The free states (06, 11) and the clearly blocked states (04, 05, 10) I do understand.

## 3. Problems, most severe first

1. **On the phone, the money facts live far from the Send button.** (01, 02, 03, 07, 08, 09 phone — worst in 08/09.) The composer only says "Next reply: paid · actual usage in USD"; the fuel left, the gauge warnings and the reservation note are below the whole "Your live brief" card. I'd fire off paid messages without seeing my balance shrink or that I'm at "Nearly used". **Fix:** put a one-line live summary at the composer ("Paid · $2.00 left · getting low") and keep the bar beside Send, with the full card below. **Major.**
2. **07's pending wording makes me fear a double charge.** Card: "Usage pending · $0.25 reserved. This is separate from settled charges." with $9.704 showing. Is the $0.25 already off that number or will it be taken again? And nothing shows which reply is pending. I could wrongly believe the reply already cost $0.25 (10 − 0.25 ≈ 9.75, very close to $9.704 I can see). **Fix:** "We set aside $0.25 from your balance while this reply runs — you pay only the real usage, the rest returns. Not an extra charge," plus a small "pending" marker on that message. **Major.**
3. **"Buy fuel" hides its terms.** (All cards; buttons in 04, 05, 10; desktop and phone.) I can't see any price, any minimum, or that buying fuel gives no extra free replies — a $50 minimum would be a nasty surprise for our budget. **Fix:** "Buy fuel — from $50. Adds paid usage only, not free replies." **Major.**
4. **"Within allowance" next to a "Fuel needed"/$0.00 state.** (04, 05; desktop and phone.) The bar says everything is within allowance while the badge, the gray box and the disabled Send say I'm out of fuel. Contradictory signals at the worst moment. **Fix:** when fuel is $0, the bar should say "No fuel left", not "Within allowance". **Minor.**
5. **The gauge doesn't fully explain itself.** (08, 09; desktop, and phone when reached.) At 80% the bar reads dark to me — I'm not certain it's the "yellow" warning. At 95.01% the red bar could read as "blocked", and nothing on screen says red is only a warning and a funded reply still works. **Fix:** label the stages plainly: "Getting low — paid replies still work" / "Nearly used — paid replies still work". **Minor.**
6. **"Add project fuel beside this chat" is wrong on the phone.** (04, 05 phone.) The fuel card is not beside the composer; it's far below, past the live brief. **Fix:** "add project fuel in the usage card below" or a button that jumps there. **Minor.**
7. **Beta wording is clunky and slightly alarming.** (01, 04, 10: "Your 42 remaining beta replies can only be used within each day's 10" — I had to read it three times. 03, 05, 10: "They do not come back.") It sounds like a punishment and repeats "a daily reset does not renew it". **Fix:** "Beta replies only count while you still have a daily free reply left," and "Beta free replies don't reset." **Minor.**
8. **"No paid fuel is allocated to Discovery."** (04, 05, 10.) "Allocated" and "Discovery" are internal words; I'd rather read "This project has no fuel money for this chat." **Minor.**
9. **Small label mismatches.** (01–05, 07: "Actual usage in USD" sits above turn counts like "0 of 10 left", not dollars. Also "replies" and "turns" are used for the same thing.) **Fix:** "Left" as the column header, and pick one word — replies or turns. **Minor.**
