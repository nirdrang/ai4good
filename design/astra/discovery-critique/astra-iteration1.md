## 2. Problems, most severe first

**Major — Finish Discovery disappears while answering.**  
**Where:** Steps 2/4, laptop; step 5, phone.  
At the scrolled answer box, the progress panel and Finish Discovery were off-screen. On completion, I was told to return to the top. I lose my place and cannot readily check how close I am to finishing.  
**Fix:** Keep a compact progress strip and Finish Discovery button fixed on-screen, disabled until all required topics are agreed.

**Major — Two navigation systems describe different next steps.**  
**Where:** Steps 2–3, laptop; phone navigation in step 5.  
The sidebar says “3 Scope document” and “4 Publish and review”; the process bar says “3 Volunteer match” and “4 PRD.” As Dana, I cannot tell which sequence to trust.  
**Fix:** Use consistent stage names and numbering everywhere. Present the review as the final part of Discovery, keeping Discovery current until confirmation.

**Major — Maintenance expectations are not explained in everyday terms.**  
**Where:** Step 3, laptop review; step 5, phone review text.  
The review says I will maintain routine changes in a “Lovable workspace.” The process bar also uses “PRD.” I am being asked to approve responsibilities without understanding the tools or skills involved.  
**Fix:** Explain what I will actually do, what help I receive, and any costs I need to understand before confirming. Expand or replace “PRD.”

**Minor — The full money picture is far from Send.**  
**Where:** Step 4, laptop; step 5, phone.  
The usage card contains all four required items, but it sits after the live brief. At the phone message box, I saw the next-reply line, not both allowances and the balance together. Checking my small budget requires extra scrolling.  
**Fix:** Move the complete, compact usage card directly above the message box, retaining both counters, fuel and next-reply mode together.

**Minor — The phone introduction delays the first question.**  
**Where:** Step 5, phone.  
The initial screen was occupied by the title, View brief, stages and progress information; the first question was below it. This makes a quick answer on the go feel slower.  
**Fix:** Shorten the introductory and progress text, leaving details in the checklist while keeping Finish Discovery visible.

**Minor — Money amounts require unnecessary interpretation.**  
**Where:** Step 4, laptop.  
Balances such as **$9.954** and **$0.499**, alongside a **$0.006** fee, made me pause to work out the cost in cents.  
**Fix:** Explain fractional-cent precision and show an easy-to-read equivalent, such as “4.6¢ charged,” with the exact breakdown available.
