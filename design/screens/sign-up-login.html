Before you can create a project
Please read this in full. It is the whole agreement, not a summary, and it is the same text every organisation on ai4good agrees to.
The ai4good Promise
We are a coordination layer, not a vendor. We introduce your organisation to a volunteer developer and we operate the tools they build with. We do not employ them, we do not supervise their work, and we make no promise that your tool will be finished, be fit for purpose, or be delivered by any date.

Fuel
Fuel is prepaid, non-cash, project-scoped credit that pays for AI compute. It can be fully consumed without producing a working deliverable. It is not refundable, cannot be withdrawn or transferred to another organisation, and has no cash value. The amount you choose to fund is your maximum exposure on that project: we will never charge you more without you funding again. Unused fuel is released to your organisation’s general balance as credit that does not expire and applies automatically to future funding.

Openness
Every project is public. The repository is MIT licensed from the first commit, and the project page — including progress, spend, and blockers — is readable by anyone. Do not put confidential material into a project. Reference files you upload are visible to ai4good and to your volunteer, so upload redacted or sample data only.

Ownership and continuity
You own the running app and its repository from the beginning; there is nothing for us to hand to you at the end. If your volunteer stops, the project reopens for a new match and your fuel stays with the project. You may stop at any point; spent fuel is not returned.

The separate Lovable cost
The app-building layer is Lovable, and your organisation pays Lovable directly — roughly $25 a month while the project runs. That is not part of fuel and we never bill it.
