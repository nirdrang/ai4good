Discovery classified this project Tier 2: the finished tool will touch personal or sensitive records. That changes what may be uploaded here, permanently.
What Tier 2 means for files
Nothing real, ever: no genuine names, addresses, health notes, case records or identifying details — not even “just one row to show the format”. Upload fixtures: made-up records in the real structure. The build and all testing run on fixtures; real data only ever enters the finished tool, inside your own accounts, after completion. ai4good and your volunteer see everything you upload here, and every project’s repository is public.
