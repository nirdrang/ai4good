How it works
Small nonprofits need small tools.
Volunteer developers build them with AI.
An intake form, a case tracker, a shift scheduler, a directory. You describe the need; a volunteer developer builds it with AI assistance; your organisation prepays the AI compute. You end up with a running app and its source code, and you keep changing it yourself by chat.
I work at a nonprofit

I’m a developer who wants to help
Connect GitHub, browse open projects, take one on.
What this is, and is not
We coordinate. We do not deliver.
ai4good is a coordination layer, not an agency and not a vendor. One volunteer developer takes one project. There is no contract with us, no service level, and no promise that your tool ships.
Fuel is prepaid credit, not cash.
Fuel pays for the AI compute that does the building. It is not cash: no refunds, no cash-out, no transfers. Unused fuel stays as credit for your organisation’s next project. You choose the amount, and the amount you choose is your maximum exposure.
Every project is open source.
The repository is public and MIT licensed from the first commit, and the project page is public while the work happens. You own the running app and the repository throughout — there is nothing to hand over at the end.
How a project runs
01
Describe the need
Plain language. No technical spec, no budget figure.
02
Discovery writes the scope
A guided chat, 5–10 turns, ends in a scope document you can edit.
03
A founder reviews it
Every project is read by a human before it goes live. You will hear back either way.
04
We match one volunteer
Matching is done by hand. You do not sift through applicants.
05
You fund fuel, the build runs
Minimum $50. Progress, spend and blockers are visible the whole way.
Before you spend anything, in plain terms. Fuel powers AI work and can be fully spent without producing a tool you can use. It is not cash-refundable and cannot be withdrawn. A $100 top-up gives you $100 of fuel — there is no fee line. Unused fuel returns to your organisation’s general balance as credit, which never expires and applies automatically at your next funding. Separately, the app-building layer (Lovable) is paid by your organisation directly to Lovable, roughly $25 a month, and is not part of fuel.
Primary action is the NGO card; the volunteer card is quiet and equal in size — neither audience is a secondary citizen. The three explainer panels answer the three things an anxious NGO asks first, in the order they ask them.
