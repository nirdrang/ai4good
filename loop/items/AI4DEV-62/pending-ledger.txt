Why this file exists
--------------------
`tests/at/expected/req-001.json` declares 16 of REQ-001's 37 P0 ids red at the loop tier, and
`tests/at/harness/expected.ts` matches such a red by SHAPE: it rebuilds the anchored prefix

    AtPending: <id> PENDING [sut-missing] -

and compares it from position 0. Everything AFTER the em dash is free. So a stub whose detail
read "todo" would satisfy `at:check`, `at:verify --expect` and CI alike, while the record said
nothing about who lands the id or when.

Nothing mechanical closes that. This file is the check that does: one line per pending id, and
every deliverable-and-leaf named below really appears in `loop/decomp/req-001.md`. A reviewer
compares the two files.

The detail strings themselves live in `tests/at/suites/req-001/_pending.ts` (the `LEAF` map) and
are attached to each id at its own `atTest` call site.

Two things this ledger does NOT claim
-------------------------------------
That a later leaf will keep its promise. The ledger names the leaf; it cannot make that leaf land
the id. What it does is make a broken promise VISIBLE - if a leaf lands and the id stays red, the
two files disagree and a reader can see which one is lying.

And, specific to the five ids these two items removed: what their green claims is narrower than
the criteria read on their own.

  AT-001.16 claims OPERATION-SURFACE isolation - one account's authority does not cross
  organisations on the admin-only action, proved by three different answers to one caller. It
  does NOT claim read isolation over drafts, ledgers or files. That breadth belongs to D5.L1,
  which the manifest blocks on this very leaf, and this tree has no read surface to leak through:
  row-level security is on with zero policies and `org_memberships` reaches no client role.

  AT-001.36's `member` seat is OPERATOR-PROVISIONED, and both bodies say so in their own
  evidence. No product path writes that role and none may - the single-seat invariant forbids

  AT-001.17's "UI absent" clause is proved by a NAMING oracle over `src/routes/` and the
  generated route tree. A deliberately renamed invite screen escapes it, as it escapes any static
  check; semantic absence stays with review.
