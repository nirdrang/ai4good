## A. First impression

I thought ai4good was offering Harbor Community Kitchen a way to get free software built by volunteers. On my phone, the bold heading and large buttons made it look straightforward, though the choice of account type took up much of the first screen. On my laptop, the dark green panel made it easier to understand the service and see who could access the charity’s information.

## C. Usability hints

1. **Tell me who can see our information before I type it.** On the first `Main` screen, I had an email box but couldn’t see the privacy explanation that was prominent on desktop. On the phone I found it later, inside the expanded terms. Put a short **Who can see our information?** link beside the email field and repeat the answer in the terms summary. “Your organisation’s private information can be seen by you, your assigned volunteer and the ai4good team. The software code is public.” [01-r2-phone-sign-up-start.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/dana/shots/01-r2-phone-sign-up-start.png) · [10-r2-phone-expanded-terms-before-consent.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/dana/shots/10-r2-phone-expanded-terms-before-consent.png) · [17-r2-desktop-sign-up-start.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/dana/shots/17-r2-desktop-sign-up-start.png)

I couldn’t tell what another member of staff should do. Put both ideas together under the heading. “Set up your organisation once. Each staff member uses their own sign-in.” Add a **My organisation already has an account** link that explains how to get access. ## D. Works

- The phone sign-up showed me signed in as Dana Okonkwo for Harbor Community Kitchen. I could read the full terms above both consent boxes before ticking them.
- Google was quicker on desktop. It filled in my name and skipped choosing a password and checking my email.
- Phone sign-in rejected my deliberately wrong password and accepted the correct one. **Forgot password?** carried my email into the reset form.
- Phone reset let me resend the link, rejected a short replacement, accepted the new password and rejected the old one. The new password also worked when I ran through the desktop reset board.
- Sign-up flagged blank fields, a malformed email and a short password. Submitting the incomplete organisation form brought the first missing field into view.

## E. If I could change only three things

1. Send me back to **Set up your organisation** if I sign in before finishing it.
2. Clear both consent boxes whenever I change the organisation name.
3. Put the privacy explanation beside the email field on the first phone screen.

## F. Scores

- **Looks 8/10.** The headings and buttons were easy to read, and the role choices took up more of my phone screen than I needed.
- **Clarity 7/10.** I could follow the main route, but going back after confirming my email made me unsure which step I was on.
- **How safe I felt giving my details 6/10.** Reading the terms before ticking either box helped, but seeing both ticks carry over to a different organisation made me hesitate.
