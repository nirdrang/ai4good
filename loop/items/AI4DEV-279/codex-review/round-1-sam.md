## C. Usability hints

1. **Explain GitHub access before either route links it.** On `Desktop` and `DesktopFinishVolunteer`, I learned from the email route that the site says it reads my public handle, top languages, repository count, and contributions and shows them to nonprofits. The screen calls the link permanent, and I found no unlink control. The simulated flow left the actual OAuth scopes unverified. Put the disclosure before authorization and use wording like “We read these public GitHub details and show them on your volunteer profile. Review the requested permissions before connecting.” Add **Disconnect GitHub** in account settings and explain which imported details it removes. [04-desktop-github-finish-form.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/04-desktop-github-finish-form.png), [52-desktop-finish-standalone.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/52-desktop-finish-standalone.png)
2. **Set expectations before I create a password.** On `Desktop`, “or with email” looked like a complete alternative. I created a password, confirmed my email, checked the agreement, and clicked **Finish sign-up**. The red message confirmed that I had to link GitHub, and I could finish only after linking it. Let email volunteers finish and browse, then ask them to connect when they take on a project that needs GitHub. If linking stays mandatory at sign-up, put “All volunteers connect GitHub, even when signing up with email” directly under the volunteer choice. [09-desktop-email-route-volunteer.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/09-desktop-email-route-volunteer.png), [14-desktop-email-finish-blocked-without-github.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/14-desktop-email-finish-blocked-without-github.png)
3. **Give the finishing screen one primary action.** Before linking on `PhoneFinishVolunteer`, **Link GitHub** and **Finish sign-up** had the same dark fill and visual weight. After linking, the green card said I was linked, but the heading still told me to link. Show **Connect GitHub** as the primary action first. Once it succeeds, change the heading to **Review and finish** and give **Finish sign-up** the primary style. Put two underlined links, **Read the Terms** and **Read the Platform Promise**, outside the agreement checkbox. [27-phone-finish-standalone.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/27-phone-finish-standalone.png), [24-phone-github-linked.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/24-phone-github-linked.png)
4. **Keep Sign in visible on the phone.** On `Main` at 390 × 844, selecting volunteer inserted the GitHub button and pushed the small **Sign in** link partly below the viewport. Scrolling it into view tucked the page title behind the sticky header. Move **Sign in** into that header and shorten the nonprofit description to “Get software built for your nonprofit” so the account-type cards take up less vertical space. [89-phone-volunteer-sign-in-link-at-fold.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/89-phone-volunteer-sign-in-link-at-fold.png), [90-phone-volunteer-sign-in-link-after-scroll.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/90-phone-volunteer-sign-in-link-after-scroll.png)
5. **Make error recovery quicker by keyboard.** On `Desktop`, pressing Enter on an empty form left focus on **Create account**, below the two errors. Move focus to the first invalid field and keep its message visible until that field is corrected. When a failed sign-in clears the password, return the field to masked mode. Let Escape mask it while it’s visible. [35-desktop-keyboard-empty-submit-errors.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/35-desktop-keyboard-empty-submit-errors.png), [76-desktop-sign-in-retry-password-still-visible.png](/tmp/claude-0/personas-signin/sam/shots/76-desktop-sign-in-retry-password-still-visible.png)

Give volunteers their own copy, such as “Confirm your email to continue volunteer sign-up.” If browsing before confirmation is intended, put a **Sign in to browse projects** link on that screen once the path works. ## D. Works

- Desktop GitHub sign-up reached the signed-in volunteer profile as Sam Rivera, `@samrivera`. Sign-out returned me to sign-in, and **Sign in with GitHub** worked on both dedicated sign-in boards.
- The email confirmation, GitHub link, and agreement steps led to a signed-in profile on desktop and phone. **Resend the email** showed feedback in the preview, and **Use a different email** returned me to an editable form.
- Tab showed a visible focus ring. Arrow keys switched roles, Space toggled the checkbox and Show/Hide, and Enter submitted the form. Switching between nonprofit and volunteer preserved my typed email and password.
- Empty and malformed inputs got specific messages. The on-screen **Back** from email confirmation restored my role and email, and the password field was blank.

## E. If I could change only three things

1. Put GitHub access and profile visibility in front of authorization, and make the connection reversible.
2. Let email volunteers finish sign-up and decide whether to link GitHub when they pick a project.
3. Make the password saved in the reset flow work on the sign-in screen it sends me to.

## F. Scores

- **Looks · 7/10.** The green and navy palette felt calm and the type was readable, but the two dark buttons competed for attention and the phone form clipped Sign in.
- **Clarity · 5/10.** I understood how to get started, but I found out what email sign-up required after confirming my address.
- **Safety · 4/10.** The late permanence warning, unverifiable OAuth permissions, and visible password retry would make me pause before using my real account.
